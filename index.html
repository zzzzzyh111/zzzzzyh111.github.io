I am a Ph.D. candidate in Robotics and Intelligent Systems at the School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Mir Feroskhan.

My research centers on embodied visual intelligence for aerial robots: learning visuomotor policies directly from monocular vision, building action-conditioned world models, and grounding vision-language instructions into flight. I am particularly interested in closing the sim-to-real gap with 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstructions and deploying learned policies on real quadrotors.

Before my Ph.D., I received an M.Eng. in Robotics and Intelligent Systems from NTU, supervised by Assoc. Prof. Chen Lv, and a B.Eng. in Flight Vehicle Propulsion Engineering from Harbin Engineering University.
